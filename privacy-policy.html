1. Introduction & Identifier Details

This Privacy Policy describes how developer Sandesh Koli ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our Android mobile application AI Job Helper (also branded as AI Career Coach, package name com.sandeshkoli.aicareercoach) (the "App").

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Information You Provide:
    • Account Data: Name and email address collected via Google Sign-In authentication.
    • Profile Data: Information you enter for career coaching, such as target job, user role, and selected skills.
    • Interview & Resume Content: Mock interview questions, user answers, AI feedback scores, and resume content created in the App.
  • Information Collected Automatically:
    • Usage Data: Feature interactions and basic crash logs for analytics and app performance.
    • Device Information: Basic device model and OS version for troubleshooting.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and optimize App features.
  • Personalize AI-generated mock interview questions and evaluation feedback.
  • Process credit purchases securely via Razorpay.
  • Display relevant advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Send FCM service notifications for daily challenges.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with third-party service providers necessary to operate the App:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase: For account authentication, Firestore database, and Cloud Functions.
  • Google Gemini API: User interview responses are processed by Google Gemini AI models to generate feedback.
  • Google AdMob: To display in-app advertisements.
  • Razorpay: For processing secure payment transactions.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable security measures (including HTTPS encryption and Firebase Security Rules) to protect your personal data.
